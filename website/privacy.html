Pitagorin Privacy Policy

The short version: no account is required, learning data stays on the device, and Pitagorin does not ask for a name or email address.

What stays on your device

Pitagorin stores settings, progress, scores, personal bests, times-table mastery, Academy history, and optional photos of handwritten work locally. Pitagorin does not upload this learning data to its own servers. Apple-controlled device backups may apply.

You can remove local data by deleting the app. No account is required to use Pitagorin.

Advertising and product analytics

This static website has no sign-in, contact form, advertising, analytics, or Pitagorin cookies.

The free app may show occasional interstitial ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Pro users do not see ads. Advertising is configured for child-directed treatment, a general content rating, and disabled ad personalisation. On physical devices, Pitagorin asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before starting ads; declining does not block learning.

Firebase Analytics helps measure aggregate use of learning areas and modes. Analytics excludes names, accounts, answers, equations, scores, progress records, purchase IDs, precise location, advertising IDs, and handwritten-work photos. Automatic screen reporting, IDFV collection, and analytics ad-personalisation are disabled.

Google may process limited technical information under its privacy policy.

Photos and purchases

Camera or photo-library access is requested only when you choose to attach handwritten work to an Academy exam. The photo stays with that local exam history.

Apple processes Pitagorin Pro payments through the App Store and StoreKit. Pitagorin receives the purchase state needed to unlock Pro, not your payment card or bank details. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Children, families, and adult learners

Pitagorin is designed with children and families in mind and is also suitable for teens and adults. The app does not ask children to create an account or provide direct contact details. Parents and guardians should review device, App Store, advertising, and backup settings with their children.

Your choices and questions

  • Decline tracking permission and continue using the learning experience.
  • Choose not to attach photos of handwritten work.
  • Delete the app to remove locally stored Pitagorin data.
  • Use the developer contact link on the Pitagorin App Store listing for privacy questions.

This page will be updated if Pitagorin’s data practices change.
