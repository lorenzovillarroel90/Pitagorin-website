Privacy at Pitagorin

A clear view of what the app handles.

Pitagorin is built for focused math practice. This policy explains what stays on the device, what third-party services may process, and what information the app does not request.

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Pitagorin is developed by Lorenzo Villarroel Perez. No account is required to use the app. The current website is a static site and does not include a contact form, advertising script, analytics script, or sign-in system.

Information stored on your device

Pitagorin stores the information needed to make practice personal and useful on the device where the app is used. This can include:

  • Learning preferences and app settings.
  • Learning progress, personal bests, scores, and Times Tables mastery.
  • Academy exam history and review information.
  • Optional photos of handwritten work attached to an Academy exam.
  • The local state used to remember a Pitagorin Pro purchase.

Pitagorin does not operate an account system for this information and does not upload this learning data to Pitagorin servers. Device backups and operating-system services are controlled by Apple and may apply separately.

Advertising

The free experience uses occasional interstitial advertising from Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to support development. Pitagorin Pro does not show ads.

Pitagorin configures advertising for child-directed treatment, a general maximum ad-content rating, and disabled ad personalisation. On a physical iPhone or iPad, the app requests App Tracking Transparency permission before starting the advertising SDK or loading ads. You can deny or restrict that permission and continue using the learning experience.

AdMob may process limited device and usage information to deliver, measure, and protect advertising under Google's policies. Pitagorin does not send children's names, answers, equations, or handwritten-work photos to the advertising service.

Product analytics

Pitagorin uses Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate product usage, such as which learning areas and modes are opened. Product analytics is limited to an allowlisted set of events and does not include names, user accounts, user IDs, child-entered content, answers, equations, scores, progress records, purchase identifiers, precise location, or advertising identifiers.

Automatic screen-view reporting, IDFV collection, and analytics ad-personalisation signals are disabled. Firebase is a third-party service and may process technical information under Google's policies.

Camera and photo library

The app requests camera or photo-library access only when someone chooses to attach a photo of handwritten work to an Academy exam. The photo is stored with that local exam history. Pitagorin does not upload that photo to its own servers.

Purchases

Pitagorin Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple handles payment details and transaction processing. Pitagorin receives the purchase state needed to unlock Pro features, not the payment card or bank details used for the transaction.

Third-party services

The app uses the following services:

  • Google Mobile Ads and Firebase privacy information.
  • Apple privacy information for App Store and StoreKit services.

Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Their policies may change, so the linked pages are the best source for their current practices.

Children, families, and adult learners

Pitagorin is designed with children and families in mind, and is also suitable for teens and adults who want to refresh basic math or keep their mental-math skills active. The app does not ask children to create accounts or provide names, email addresses, or other direct contact details. Parents and guardians should review device, App Store, advertising, and backup settings with their children.

Changes and questions

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new date and a description of the change. For privacy questions, use the developer contact route available from the Pitagorin App Store listing.
