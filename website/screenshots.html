The real experience

Clear enough to learn. Bright enough to return.

These are real Pitagorin screens. Each one pairs a focused mathematical task with inviting visual cues and a clear next action.

Four learning moments

From quick recall to guided reasoning.

The interface changes with the goal: energetic for a challenge, calm for learning, focused for recall, and explicit for algebraic reasoning.

Designed for focus

The decoration has a job, too.

Pitagorin uses colour to welcome learners into the activity, while the work area stays spacious and legible. Questions, feedback, and controls remain the visual priority.

Readable questions

Large mathematical expressions and generous spacing keep the task easy to locate.

Clear choices

Each screen limits competing actions so learners know what to do next.

Support on demand

Learning help appears when it is useful instead of interrupting every attempt.

Progress you can see

Scores, timing, mastery, and history give practice a concrete direction.
