Inside Pitagorin

Six paths. One clear purpose.

Pitagorin meets learners where they are: a fast arithmetic game, a calm explanation, focused times-table practice, a self-built exam, a reasoning puzzle, or guided algebra.

Compare the modesSee the real app

Choose your route

A mode for every kind of practice.

Each feature is designed around a distinct learning need, so learners can choose intensity, topic, and level of support without losing focus.

Fluency

Arithmetic games

Practise addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, decimals, percentages, powers, roots, and more. Easy, Medium, and Hard levels keep the challenge appropriate.

Understanding

Learning Game

Work without a timer, type an answer, and open operation-specific visual hints. It is the calmest route for learning a method before adding speed.

Recall

Times Tables

Learn tables 1–12 in focused sessions. Pitagorin Pro adds Smart Review for weaker facts and a short fluency sprint.

Independent practice

Academy

Build an exam by choosing operations and difficulty. Save the result, attach optional handwritten work, and revisit the history with tutorial playback.

Reasoning

Math Games

Reach a target number or balance both sides of an equation. Hints, undo, and replayable challenges make strategy visible.

Next step

Guided Algebra

Follow a grade-organised path through foundations, equations, proportional thinking, linear relationships, systems, exponents, and functions.

The learning loop

Try. Understand. Try again.

Fast feedback keeps momentum, while tutorials make room for genuine understanding. The two belong together.

  • Choose an operation, table, topic, or puzzle
  • Adjust difficulty to the learner
  • Use visual support when the method is unclear
  • Return to progress, mastery, and personal bests

How a session works

A simple route into maths.

There is no single correct way to practise. Pitagorin keeps the next action understandable for an independent child or an adult returning to maths.

ChoosePick a topic or game that matches today’s goal.
TryWork at a comfortable level and pace.
UnderstandOpen a hint or tutorial when needed.
ReturnBuild mastery or set a new personal best.
