For families and lifelong learners

Support that helps children stay curious.

Pitagorin gives children room to practice independently while also offering teens and adults a welcoming way to refresh basic math, build mental-math fluency, and keep their skills active.

🌱

Start at the right level

Age-aligned operations and three difficulty levels make it easier to begin with a manageable challenge and grow from there.

📝

See the method

Tutorials explain reliable paper methods step by step, including carries, borrowing, fractions, roots, and order of operations.

🏆

Celebrate progress

Personal bests, mastery, exam history, and focused challenge scores turn improvement into something children can see.

A clear learning path

From first facts to Algebra.

Pitagorin covers the foundations children use every day and the ideas they meet later in school. Families can choose quick arithmetic practice, a Times Tables session, Academy exams, or an Algebra topic.

What children can practice

  • Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division
  • Fractions, decimals, percentages, powers, and roots
  • Times Tables from 1 through 12
  • Equations, ratios, functions, and linear relationships

Good to know

Designed with care.

Pitagorin is free to download with optional Pro features. The app supports larger text, dark appearance, reduced motion, and VoiceOver-friendly descriptions for core mathematical visuals.
