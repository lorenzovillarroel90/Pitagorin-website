Straight answers

What would you like to know?

A concise guide to Pitagorin’s learners, maths topics, learning support, devices, privacy, advertising, and optional Pro features.

What age is Pitagorin for?

Pitagorin is designed primarily for children ages 5–13. Its difficulty choices and broader topics also make it useful for teens and adults who want to refresh basic maths.

What maths can learners practise?

Learners can practise addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, decimals, percentages, powers, roots, times tables 1–12, equations, ratios, functions, and linear relationships.

Does Pitagorin explain how to solve a problem?

Yes. The Learning Game includes visual hints and paper-method tutorials, and Algebra offers guided solving that explains one step at a time.

Which devices does Pitagorin support?

Pitagorin is available for iPhone and iPad. It requires iOS 18 or later on iPhone and iPadOS 18 or later on iPad.

Can teens and adults use Pitagorin?

Yes. Teens and adults can use short arithmetic, times-table, mental-maths, and algebra sessions to refresh skills and keep their minds active.

Does Pitagorin require an account?

No. Pitagorin does not require an account, name, or email address. Learning progress is stored locally on the device.

Is Pitagorin free?

Pitagorin is free to download. Optional Pitagorin Pro features are available through in-app purchases.

Does Pitagorin show advertising?

The free version may show occasional interstitial ads. Pitagorin Pro does not show ads. See the Privacy Policy for details.
