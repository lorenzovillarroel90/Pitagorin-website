Helpful answers

Questions parents ask first.

A quick guide to Pitagorin's learning paths, practice modes, and the ideas behind the app.

What age is Pitagorin for?

Pitagorin is designed for children ages 5–13, with different operations and difficulty levels for growing learners.

What can children practice?

Children can practice arithmetic, Times Tables from 1 through 12, fractions, decimals, percentages, powers, roots, equations, ratios, functions, and linear relationships.

Does Pitagorin explain the method?

Yes. Paper-method tutorials and visual hints show the thinking behind an answer, including guided Algebra solving.

Which devices does Pitagorin support?

The current website presents Pitagorin for iPhone and iPad. The final App Store link and compatibility details will be added before launch.
